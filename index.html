Engineering Highlights

Built for Precision. Tuned for Power.

Every layer of Mactivity was written from scratch in native Swift 6 and AppKit to eliminate the flaws of conventional monitors.

Zero Jitter

Pixel-Stabilized Menu Bar Pill

Unlike other menu bar tools that shrink or stretch when CPU percentages jump from 9% to 100%, Mactivity enforces fixed-width geometry for every metric mode. Your adjacent menu bar icons stay perfectly anchored.

⊚ 14% ↓24K
⊚ 99% ↓24K
Same exact 92pt width. Zero shift.
Auxiliary Overlay

Floats Over Fullscreen Apps

Equipped with .canJoinAllSpaces and .fullScreenAuxiliary window server behaviors. Mactivity floats gracefully above full-screen Xcode, Safari, or IDE spaces without triggering annoying desktop transitions.

Click Outside to Close ESC to Dismiss Status Bar Level
Zero Shell Overhead

Direct Mach & IOKit Telemetry

No /bin/sh -c processes. No spawning of top or pmset commands. We probe C-level kernel structures (host_statistics64, vm_statistics64) directly from Swift.

Mactivity CPU < 0.3%
Shell-based Apps 3% – 8%

# 2. Build the optimized native application bundle

$ ./build.sh

# 3. Launch Mactivity into your Menu Bar!

$ open build/Mactivity.app
